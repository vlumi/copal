# Plan: a deeper Gondwana

Gondwana Life Insurance runs, the survey reads it, and a year of it runs from a seed. As a batch system it is convincing: the banks' transfer file is the real layout field for field, the debit on the 27th with its results days later and a second debit for an arrear is how the trade collects, and the shop's history shows in its files. As a life insurer it is thin. A policy is one product with one sum; the loan balance on the master is deducted from benefits and nothing ever lends or collects it; a benefit is judged by the policy's state on the day the request is keyed, not on the day of the event; and the policyholders' notices the [third specimen's plan](third-specimen.md) names were never written. This plan fills that in, the gaps a reader who knows the business would see first.

Read [third-specimen.md](third-specimen.md) first; its ground rules apply. Gondwana is fiction, it runs, it knows nothing of the survey, and what it shows the survey to be coupled to is fixed generically. Every part updates the March fixture and its goldens, the blueprints and the oracle's cases, the generator and the year, and the operations guide.

## Parts

1. **The cash value, called.** A surrender's cash value is worked out in GLD030 alone, from a table it loads itself. Loans, the automatic premium loan and the anniversaries all need it, so it becomes a called module that reads its own table, GLC030, as the third specimen's plan had it.

   As built: GLC030 loads the surrender value table on its first call and answers from it after; GLD030 calls it, and the March fixture's outputs do not change. It showed two couplings, both to programs whose files are all their own. The survey bound a step's datasets to the files of the step's program only, so the daily job stopped reading CSVTB: a step now binds the files of the programs its program calls, at any depth, and names the program called. The oracle gave a run the files of the program run only: a run's files are now those of every program it can reach, a called program's file is an input of the search, and a condition of a called program follows its values to that file's fields. The cases of GLD030 reach 29 of its 114 rules where they reached 33 of 117: the four were the table's loading, which every run did and now only a surrender paid does, and no case pays one yet, the search still making no policy in the state a request needs.
2. **Policy loans.** A branch's request lends against the cash value, up to a limit, or takes a repayment; the anniversary adds the year's interest to the balance, and a balance past the cash value ends the policy. The **automatic premium loan** follows: when a policy's grace runs out and its cash value covers the arrears, the premium is lent rather than the policy lapsed.

   As built: the master keeps, beside the balance, the day interest was last added to it, in seven bytes of its filler. GLC040 works out a loan's interest, three percent a year by the day, and every step that touches a balance adds it first. GLD070, a new daily step, takes the branches' loans and repayments from `LONREQ`: a loan within eight tenths of the cash value to a policy in force, paid to the policyholder's account, a repayment of no more than the balance. GLD020 adds the interest at each anniversary, lapses a policy whose balance has passed its cash value, and takes the loan off a maturity benefit, which paid the sum whole before. GLD030 takes the interest to the day with the loan off a death benefit or a surrender. GLD060 lends the unpaid premiums of a policy whose grace has run out when its cash value covers them, the automatic premium loan, and lapses only the rest. The March fixture lends, repays, rejects, adds interest, lapses one policy over its cash value and saves one from lapse. The generator makes up the branches' loan requests, and the insurer's day counts them. The survey reads the new step and the two called programs with no change to it; the oracle's cases reach 61 of GLD070's 102 rules, 39 of GLD060's 77 and 44 of GLD020's 105.

   The same year from the same seed: 51 loans of 16,140,000 yen and 44 repayments, 12 requests rejected; 77 policies kept in force by the automatic premium loan, 7,967,112 yen of premiums lent, and 33 lapsed; no balance passed its cash value; the year ends with 1,904 in force, 41 in grace and 126 lapsed, where it ended with 1,865, 31 and 178. One thing stands as the [third specimen's plan](third-specimen.md) found it: when the debit is late the lapse step still runs before its results arrive, so a policy may now have premiums lent that the debit then collects, and be paid ahead by them.
3. **Benefits by the event.** A benefit is judged by the policy's state on the event date: a death before the lapse is paid though the request comes after it, with the unpaid premiums deducted, and an event after the business date is rejected. A suicide within the exclusion period of a contract or a reinstatement pays the cash value, not the sum. A medical policy pays no death benefit of its sum.

   As built: GLD030 pays a death or a hospitalization when the policy was covered on the day of the event, covered now or lapsed since, and rejects an event after the business date. A death benefit is less the premiums unpaid to the month of the death, booked as premium income. The branches' request carries a cause, in a byte of its filler, and the master the day of the last reinstatement, in seven of its own, which GLD040 writes: a suicide within three years of the later of that day and the contract pays the cash value with the dividends, less the loan. A death claimed on a medical policy is rejected, where it paid a thousand days' benefit. The March fixture pays a death of 2018 on a policy that lapsed a month after it, less two months' premiums, and a suicide in a policy's second year. The same year from the same seed pays 94 benefits and rejects 18 requests: four of them deaths on medical policies, two events dated ahead, and one for a policy not covered on the day of the event. The oracle's cases reach 29 of GLD030's rules, now 145, as before: none pays a benefit yet, the search still making no policy in the state a request needs.
4. **Benefit history.** A file of the benefits paid, by policy: hospitalization days are limited by stay and in total across the policy's life, a stay claimed twice is rejected, and a surgery benefit is a multiple of the daily one.

   As built: `BENHST`, an indexed file keyed by policy number and a number within the policy, which GLD030 creates on its first run, reads through for a policy with `START` and `READ NEXT`, and adds to for every hospitalization and surgery it pays. A stay is paid for 120 days at most and a policy for 1,000 in all, and a second request of the same kind for the same day is rejected. A surgery, a new kind of request, pays ten, twenty or forty times the daily benefit, the multiple keyed in the field the days are in. GLD090 unloads the history beside the master, so the fixture's goldens and the console show it. The March fixture pays a surgery and a stay of 200 days for 120, and rejects a stay sent twice and a multiple of fifteen. The same year from the same seed keeps 51 stays and 16 surgeries, and rejects three requests keyed twice. The survey reads the second indexed file, with its group key, with no change to it, and the oracle loads and unloads it; its cases reach 31 of GLD030's 172 rules.
5. **Notices.** The annual run prints the policyholders' notices, and an October run the premium certificates for the year's tax deduction, both with the Report Writer.

   As built: the certificates need the premiums a policy paid in the calendar year, which the master does not keep, so every premium collected is now kept in a payment history, `PRMHST`, an indexed file keyed by policy number and a number within the policy. A called module, GLC050, keeps the file open and writes it, and new business, the debit results, the reinstatement and the automatic premium loan call it through a procedure copybook; the survey bound the file to all four steps through the call. GLY030, in a batch file of its own run on October's first business day, reads the history through with a control break on the policy and writes a certificate a policy as a record for the printer, the amount paid so far and what the year will come to, and is not a Report Writer program: a printer takes data, not pages. GLY020, in the annual run, is one: a page a policy, the policy in a control heading, a line a coverage in force and their premiums summed in the control footing. The March fixture runs the certificates on the 31st, out of season, to have them under test: 21 certificates and 22 notices. The generated year makes 1,735 certificates in October from 8,497 payments, and 1,948 notices at its end.
6. **Collection.** An annual payer whose debit failed is debited again the next month, as a monthly payer is. A half-yearly mode. The agent's results of a debit stopped by the insurer, and of any other reason.

   As built: GLM010 bills an annual or a half-yearly payer when the next premium, a year or half a year after the paid-to month, is due by the billing month, in place of the contract month alone, so a debit that failed is sent again the month after, before the grace runs out. The half-yearly mode, six months' premium less three percent, runs through new business, the term renewal, the billing, the results, the reinstatement's arrears, the automatic premium loan and the unpaid premiums of a death benefit. The banks' file has its result codes 8 and 9, which GLD050 took for failures already and now names. The March fixture bills a half-yearly payer and an annual one in grace since February, and its results, made again from the billing's file, carry the two codes. The same year from the same seed, its payers drawn anew, 1,976 monthly, 361 annual and 110 half-yearly at the year's end: 63 policies kept by the automatic premium loan and 38 lapsed; eight debits the insurer stopped and thirteen for other reasons. It also shows nine debits rejected for their amount, each a term policy renewed at a new premium between the billing and the debit's results, which no part of this plan changes.
7. **The calendar.** The insurer keeps the national holidays and the year-end closure: the debit day, the results day and the month's last business day move with them.

   As built: the insurer's calendar, in the Go that stands in for its scheduler, knows the national holidays as the law has had them since 2020, those of a fixed day, those of a Monday, the equinoxes, the day kept for one that fell on a Sunday and the day between two, and the banks' closure from December 31 to January 3. No program changes: none reads the clock, and the anniversary step already takes every anniversary since the last business date. The fiscal year from April 1, 2026 has 243 business days where it had 261, so `make gondwana-days N=243` runs it; December closes on the 30th, and the daily run of January 4 takes five days' anniversaries. The same year from the same seed ends with 1,865 policies in force, 40 in grace and 127 lapsed, 65 kept by the automatic premium loan and 32 lapsed in it, every step ending with 0 or 4.
8. **Riders.** A policy is a main contract and its riders, each with its own sum, premium and term: a coverage file beside the master. Hospitalization becomes a rider, as it is sold, and the premium the sum of the coverages'. This changes the master and every golden file, and has a plan of its own when it starts.

   As built, by [its plan](gondwana-riders.md): `COVMST`, indexed by policy number and coverage number, holds the main contract as coverage 01 and up to two riders, a term rider and a hospitalization rider, both renewed every ten years at the attained age. The master keeps its product, sum and maturity date, the main contract's, and its premium is the total of the coverages in force, so billing, collection, lapse and loans did not change. New business writes the coverages, the anniversary renews or expires each at its own maturity date, a death pays the main contract and the term rider, a hospitalization is paid by the medical policy or the rider, whichever was in force on the day of the event, and a branch can cancel a rider. The medical policy stays a product of its own beside the rider. The March fixture has riders on eight policies, and its debit results were rebuilt from the new request. A premium that changes between the billing and the debit's results, which was a term policy's renewal only, now comes from a rider's renewal, expiry or cancellation too: 43 debits of the generated year were rejected for their amount, and one of the fixture's.

   Found later and fixed: the billing now leaves the number of premiums billed and the premium of the day on the master, in its last spare bytes, and the results are judged against those, so a debit for what was billed is taken whatever the premium has become since, and cleared, so the same result is not taken twice. No debit of the generated year or of the fixture is rejected for its amount.

   The survey read the new file without a change. The record library reads the first occurrence of a repeated field only, so the generator writes an application's second rider by copying.
9. **The books.** A journal entry has both its sides. Reserves by entry age and sex as well as product and year. Commissions by the agent's grade, charged back when a policy lapses or is surrendered in its first two years.

   As built, in three steps. A journal line is a whole entry: it names its contra account, in bytes taken from its filler, and the account is posted on the line's side and the contra on the other. Every program that books sets it, the bank account for what is collected and paid, the loan for interest, the liabilities for reserves, dividends and commissions; the automatic premium loan, which wrote the loan and the premium as two lines, writes one. GLD080, a new last step of the daily run, the close and the annual run, posts the day's journal by account and prints the trial balance; a line with no contra, or an account it does not know, is counted and ends it with 4. The reserve table has a factor by product, sex, entry age band and policy year, for the riders too, and the close adds up the reserves of a policy's coverages in force, each by the age it is rated at. Commissions are a tenth more for an agent of the second grade and a fifth more for one of the third, and a policy that lapses or is surrendered in its first or second policy year has forty percent of its first year's premiums charged back in that month's close, from the contract month and the paid-to month on the master, with no need of the payment history. The March fixture gains a surrender in a policy's second year, charged back 51,840 yen. In the generated year the trial balance agrees on each of the 243 days, with no line unposted, and the commissions come to 66.6 million yen with 5.1 million charged back.

Each part one pull request or more. Smaller things that fit a part go with it: a reinstatement within three years with interest on the arrears, a Heisei year past 31 on a date written before the era changed, a policyholder who is not the insured, a postal code.

Done when: every program reads or writes what a life insurer's would, a reader who knows the business finds no field on the master that nothing keeps, and the survey's blueprints and cases of it are as right as before.
