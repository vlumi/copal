# Plan: a deeper Gondwana

Gondwana Life Insurance runs, the survey reads it, and a year of it runs from a seed. As a batch system it is convincing: the banks' transfer file is the real layout field for field, the debit on the 27th with its results days later and a second debit for an arrear is how the trade collects, and the shop's history shows in its files. As a life insurer it is thin. A policy is one product with one sum; the loan balance on the master is deducted from benefits and nothing ever lends or collects it; a benefit is judged by the policy's state on the day the request is keyed, not on the day of the event; and the policyholders' notices the [third specimen's plan](third-specimen.md) names were never written. This plan fills that in, the gaps a reader who knows the business would see first.

Read [third-specimen.md](third-specimen.md) first; its ground rules apply. Gondwana is fiction, it runs, it knows nothing of the survey, and what it shows the survey to be coupled to is fixed generically. Every part updates the March fixture and its goldens, the blueprints and the oracle's cases, the generator and the year, and the operations guide.

## Parts

1. **The cash value, called.** A surrender's cash value is worked out in GLD030 alone, from a table it loads itself. Loans, the automatic premium loan and the anniversaries all need it, so it becomes a called module that reads its own table, GLC030, as the third specimen's plan had it.

   As built: GLC030 loads the surrender value table on its first call and answers from it after; GLD030 calls it, and the March fixture's outputs do not change. It showed two couplings, both to programs whose files are all their own. The survey bound a step's datasets to the files of the step's program only, so the daily job stopped reading CSVTB: a step now binds the files of the programs its program calls, at any depth, and names the program called. The oracle gave a run the files of the program run only: a run's files are now those of every program it can reach, a called program's file is an input of the search, and a condition of a called program follows its values to that file's fields. The cases of GLD030 reach 29 of its 114 rules where they reached 33 of 117: the four were the table's loading, which every run did and now only a surrender paid does, and no case pays one yet, the search still making no policy in the state a request needs.
2. **Policy loans.** A branch's request lends against the cash value, up to a limit, or takes a repayment; the anniversary adds the year's interest to the balance, and a balance past the cash value ends the policy. The **automatic premium loan** follows: when a policy's grace runs out and its cash value covers the arrears, the premium is lent rather than the policy lapsed.

   As built: the master keeps, beside the balance, the day interest was last added to it, in seven bytes of its filler. GLC040 works out a loan's interest, three percent a year by the day, and every step that touches a balance adds it first. GLD070, a new daily step, takes the branches' loans and repayments from `LONREQ`: a loan within eight tenths of the cash value to a policy in force, paid to the policyholder's account, a repayment of no more than the balance. GLD020 adds the interest at each anniversary, lapses a policy whose balance has passed its cash value, and takes the loan off a maturity benefit, which paid the sum whole before. GLD030 takes the interest to the day with the loan off a death benefit or a surrender. GLD060 lends the unpaid premiums of a policy whose grace has run out when its cash value covers them, the automatic premium loan, and lapses only the rest. The March fixture lends, repays, rejects, adds interest, lapses one policy over its cash value and saves one from lapse. The generator makes up the branches' loan requests, and the insurer's day counts them. The survey reads the new step and the two called programs with no change to it; the oracle's cases reach 61 of GLD070's 102 rules, 39 of GLD060's 77 and 44 of GLD020's 105.

   The same year from the same seed: 51 loans of 16,140,000 yen and 44 repayments, 12 requests rejected; 77 policies kept in force by the automatic premium loan, 7,967,112 yen of premiums lent, and 33 lapsed; no balance passed its cash value; the year ends with 1,904 in force, 41 in grace and 126 lapsed, where it ended with 1,865, 31 and 178. One thing stands as the [third specimen's plan](third-specimen.md) found it: when the debit is late the lapse step still runs before its results arrive, so a policy may now have premiums lent that the debit then collects, and be paid ahead by them.
3. **Benefits by the event.** A benefit is judged by the policy's state on the event date: a death before the lapse is paid though the request comes after it, with the unpaid premiums deducted, and an event after the business date is rejected. A death within the exclusion period of a contract or a reinstatement returns the premiums, not the sum. A medical policy pays no death benefit of its sum.
4. **Benefit history.** A file of the benefits paid, by policy: hospitalization days are limited by stay and in total across the policy's life, a stay claimed twice is rejected, and a surgery benefit is a multiple of the daily one.
5. **Notices.** The annual run prints the policyholders' notices, and an October run the premium certificates for the year's tax deduction, both with the Report Writer.
6. **Collection.** An annual payer whose debit failed is debited again the next month, as a monthly payer is. A half-yearly mode. The agent's results of a debit stopped by the insurer, and of any other reason.
7. **The calendar.** The insurer keeps the national holidays and the year-end closure: the debit day, the results day and the month's last business day move with them.
8. **Riders.** A policy is a main contract and its riders, each with its own sum, premium and term: a coverage file beside the master. Hospitalization becomes a rider, as it is sold, and the premium the sum of the coverages'. This changes the master and every golden file, and has a plan of its own when it starts.
9. **The books.** A journal entry has both its sides. Reserves by entry age and sex as well as product and year. Commissions by the agent's grade, charged back when a policy lapses or is surrendered in its first two years.

Each part one pull request or more. Smaller things that fit a part go with it: a reinstatement within three years with interest on the arrears, a Heisei year past 31 on a date written before the era changed, a policyholder who is not the insured, a postal code.

Done when: every program reads or writes what a life insurer's would, a reader who knows the business finds no field on the master that nothing keeps, and the survey's blueprints and cases of it are as right as before.
