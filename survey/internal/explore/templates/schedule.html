The order of the jobs

Derived from the generations of the datasets the jobs write and read: a job that writes a new generation, NAME(+1), runs before a job that reads the current one, NAME(0), an earlier one, NAME(-1), or every generation by its name. The survey derives the order from the data and cannot know the calendar: how often a job runs, on which days, or that one runs only once, is in the operations guide, not in the files.
