State machines

For each code field, its states are its condition names. A transition is a path through a paragraph's decisions that finds the field in one state, by its condition name or its value, and sets it to another, by SET or by MOVE of a value, when the path's other conditions are as the When column says: for a field of reasons, such as a rejection's, the system's validations; a path that sets a state without finding the field in one starts from any state, and so does a paragraph that decides nothing. The decisions of one paragraph are not followed into another: a state tested in one paragraph and set in the paragraph it performs is drawn from any state.
