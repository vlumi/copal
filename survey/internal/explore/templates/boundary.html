The boundary

The system's contracts with the outside: the datasets that come in, those that go out, the reports its job steps print, and the in-stream data its programs ACCEPT, with what they DISPLAY in reply. The survey sees the files and the streams, not what is on the other end: the branches' systems that send the day's transactions and the server around AUTHSRV that passes requests in are outside it.
