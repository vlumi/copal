Design views

The system as the business sees it, rather than as the code is organized.

  • Entities: the records the copybooks describe, their datasets, who reads and writes each field, and the state machines of their codes.
  • Ownership: the programs grouped by the entities they write, a first draft of a service decomposition.
  • The boundary: the system's contracts with the outside, its input and output files, reports and streams.
  • Forward lineage: on any field's page, follow where its values go, through every program and job step to the reports, files and replies they reach.
  • The order of the jobs: which job runs before which, derived from the generations they write and read.
