# Operations guide

Gondwana Life Insurance, Systems Department. Revision of 2026-11. Supersedes the 2026-10 edition; a journal line carries both its accounts and the day has a trial balance, the reserve is by coverage, and commissions follow the agent's grade and are charged back; the payment history, the policyholders' notices and the premium certificates are new; policy loans, their interest and the automatic premium loan are described for the first time, a benefit is judged by the day of its event, and a policy has riders, kept with its main contract in the coverage file.

## The runs

The system holds the policies in an indexed master, `MASTER\POLMST.IDX`, keyed by policy number, their coverages in a second, `MASTER\COVMST.IDX`, keyed by policy number and coverage number, and a control record, `MASTER\GLSCTL.BIN`, with the business date it stands at and the next payment and journal numbers. Dates in the master are in the era, `GYYMMDD`: `S` for Showa, `H` for Heisei, `R` for Reiwa, then the year of the era, so `R090301` is March 1, 2027. The paid-to month is the exception, `YYYYMM` in the Western calendar, as the collection agent's files have it. Amounts are whole yen. The business date is the date the batch file is given, as `YYYYMMDD`; no program reads the clock.

The batch files are in `scripts\`, and the scheduler starts them with the programs from `%GLS_BIN%`, the other batch files from `%GLS_SCRIPTS%` and the files from `%GLS_DATA%`:

- `OPEN.BAT DATE`, once, when the system opens: loads the policy extract into the master and the coverage extract, `IN\COVEXT.TXT`, into the coverage file, and writes the control record. A coverage of a policy the extract does not have is left out and counted.
- `DAILY.BAT DATE`, every business day: Monday to Friday, national holidays and December 31 to January 3 apart. The scheduler keeps the calendar; the programs take the date they are given.
- `BILLING.BAT DATE`, once a month before the debit day, the 27th or the business day after it: the transfer request to the collection agent.
- `RESULTS.BAT DATE`, on the day the agent's results arrive, a few business days after the debit, before the daily run.
- `CLOSE.BAT DATE`, on the last business day of the month, after the daily run.
- `ANNUAL.BAT DATE`, on March 31, after the close: the fiscal year ends.
- `CERT.BAT DATE`, on the first business day of October, after the daily run: the premium certificates for the calendar year.

## Coverages

A policy is a main contract and up to two riders, each a coverage in the coverage file: number 01 the main contract, 02 and 03 the riders. A coverage has its product, amount, premium, contract date, term and maturity date, the age its premium is rated at, its state and the day it ended. The master's product, sum, term and maturity date are the main contract's and repeat coverage 01; its premium is the total of the coverages in force, and that total is what is billed, collected and lent. The riders are the term rider, TR, a death benefit for ten years, and the hospitalization rider, HR, the medical policy's benefits for ten years; both are renewed at the attained age until it is past the product's maximum. A rider is sold with the policy, is never of the main contract's kind, and has no cash value. A coverage's state is its own: in force, expired at its term's end, cancelled, or ended by a death. A lapsed policy's coverages stay in force, and the policy's state comes first.

## The daily run

1. **GLD010** reads the sales system's new policies, `IN\NEWPOL.DATE.TXT`, whose dates are Western, and registers them: the product must be sold, the policy number new, the payment mode monthly, annual or half-yearly, the contract and birth dates dates of an era, the sex 1 or 2, the insurance age at the contract date within the product's range, the sum insured within its range, and a rate on the table. Each rider on the application is checked the same way, against its own product, and must be a rider product, not of the main contract's kind and not the other rider's product; one rider refused refuses the policy. The insurance age is the age in full years plus one when six months or more of the next have passed. The premium is the rate per 1,000 yen of the sum, monthly, truncated to the yen; an annual premium is twelve months less five percent, a half-yearly one six months less three. The first premium was collected with the application, so the policy is paid to its contract month and the premium is booked. The main contract and the riders are written to the coverage file, and the policy's premium is their total. A policy that fails goes to `OUT\NEWREJ.DATE.TXT` with a reason code.
2. **GLD020** moves the policy year on for every covered policy whose anniversary, the contract's day of the year, has come since the control record's business date, the last the unload moved it to, so that a weekend's anniversaries are taken on the Monday; on the day the system opens, and on a rerun, the day's own. A policy contracted that day has no anniversary yet. A policy with a loan has the interest to the anniversary added to its balance, and lapses if the balance is then more than its cash value. When the anniversary is an endowment's maturity date it pays the sum insured plus the accumulated dividends less any policy loan and marks the policy matured; when it is a term policy's it renews the policy at the insurance age attained on the anniversary, with a new premium and ten more years from it, or ends it without payment when that age is past the product's maximum. A rider whose maturity date the anniversary is, is renewed the same way or expires, and the policy goes on at the premium of what is left; when the main contract matures or ends, its riders expire with it.
3. **GLD030** pays the branches' benefit requests, `IN\CLMREQ.DATE.TXT`, whose event dates are in the era: a death benefit, the amounts of the main contract and the term rider plus dividends less any policy loan and less the premiums unpaid, a monthly payer's from the month after the paid-to month to the month of the death and an annual payer's one when the policy is not in force, after which the policy is paid out; a suicide within three years of the contract or of the last reinstatement pays the cash value in its place, and a policy with no coverage but a medical one has no death benefit; a hospitalization benefit, for a medical policy or a policy with the hospitalization rider, a daily benefit of a thousandth of that coverage's amount for up to 120 days a stay and 1,000 days in all, not within the product's waiting days after the coverage's contract date; a surgery benefit, likewise, of ten, twenty or forty times the daily benefit, the multiple keyed where the days are; or a surrender, the cash value per 1,000 yen by product, entry age band and policy year, less the loan, which a lapsed policy may still take; riders add nothing to it. A death and a surrender end every coverage. A loan is taken off with its interest to the day. Every hospitalization and surgery paid is kept in the benefit history, `MASTER\BENHST.IDX`, indexed by policy number and a number within the policy, and a second request of the same kind for the same day is rejected. A death, a hospitalization or a surgery is paid when the policy was covered on the day of the event, covered now or lapsed since, and by the coverages in force that day: in force now, or ended since. A request for a policy not covered then, or whose event is before the contract or after the business date, is rejected to `OUT\CLMREJ.DATE.TXT`.
4. **GLD040** applies the branches' changes, `IN\MNTREQ.DATE.TXT`: an address, a bank account, a name with its kana, a beneficiary, or a reinstatement of a policy lapsed within the year, which takes the arrears paid at the counter, premiums from the month after the paid-to month to this one, puts the policy back in force and notes the day on it; or the cancellation of a rider of a policy in force, by its coverage number, which ends the rider and takes its premium off the policy's. Rejects go to `OUT\MNTREJ.DATE.TXT`.
5. **GLD060** lapses the policies in grace whose grace has run out: the grace runs from the debit that failed to the end of the month after. A policy whose cash value covers its loan and the unpaid premiums, a monthly payer's to the month the grace ended and an annual payer's one, is not lapsed: the premiums are lent, the automatic premium loan, the policy is paid to that month and back in force.
6. **GLD070** takes the branches' loan requests, `IN\LONREQ.DATE.TXT`: a loan to a policy in force, paid to the policyholder's account, as long as the balance stays within eight tenths of the cash value, or a repayment, of no more than the balance. Rejects go to `OUT\LONREJ.DATE.TXT`.
7. **GLD080** prints the day's trial balance from the journal, as below.
8. **UNLOAD** runs `UNLOAD.BAT`: **GLD090** unloads the master to `OUT\POLMST.DATE.TXT` for the sales system and the accounts, the benefit history to `OUT\BENHST.DATE.TXT` and the coverage file to `OUT\COVMST.DATE.TXT`, and moves the control record's business date on.

A loan bears three percent a year, by the day. The master holds the balance and the day interest was last added to it; every step that touches the balance adds the interest to that day first. The cash value is worked out by **GLC030**, which reads `MASTER\CSVTB.TXT` itself, and the interest by **GLC040**; the steps call them.

Every program that books anything appends to the day's journal, `OUT\GLJRNL.DATE.TXT`, and every payment to `OUT\PAYOUT.DATE.TXT`, numbered from the control record. A journal line is a whole entry: its account, posted on the line's side, debit or credit, and its contra account, posted on the other, for the same amount. A premium collected credits premium income against the bank account, a benefit debits its expense against it, interest added to a loan credits interest income against the loan, a premium the automatic premium loan lends debits the loan against premium income, a reserve is booked against the reserve liability, a dividend against the dividend reserve and a commission against commissions payable. **GLD080** ends the daily run, the close and the annual run with the day's trial balance, `RPT\GLD080.DATE.RPT`: the day's journal posted by account, debits and credits, which agree; the last run of a day is the day's.

## Billing, results and the close

**GLM010** selects the policies to debit on the 27th: monthly payers paid to before the billing month, and annual and half-yearly payers whose next premium, a year or half a year after the paid-to month, is due by it, so that one whose debit failed is debited again the month after; a monthly payer in arrears is debited for two months at most. It writes the request in the banks' fixed-length layout, `OUT\PRMREQ.DATE.DAT`, 120 bytes a record with no delimiters, a header, the debits sorted by bank, branch and account, a trailer with the counts and the amount, and an end record, and notes the billing month in the control record so that a month is not billed twice. It leaves on each policy billed how many premiums it billed and the premium of that day.

**GLD050** reads the agent's results, `IN\PRMRES.DATE.DAT`, in the same layout with the result filled in. It holds the debits until the trailer and applies nothing if the counts or amounts disagree. A debit must be for what was billed, the premium of the billing day times the premiums billed, whatever a renewal or a rider's end has made the premium since; any other amount, or a result for a policy with nothing billed, is rejected. A debit that went through moves the paid-to month on, one month or two, a year or half a year, clears the arrears, and puts a policy in grace back in force; one that failed, for lack of funds, a closed account, a refusal, a missing mandate, a stop of the insurer's own or any other reason the agent gives, counts an arrear and puts a policy in force into grace from that day. Results for policies no longer paying, matured, paid out or surrendered since the billing, are rejected to `OUT\RESREJ.DATE.TXT`.

**GLM020** works out each covered policy's reserve at the month's end, the total of the reserves of its coverages in force, each by its product, the insured's sex, the band of the age the coverage is rated at and the policy year, per 1,000 yen of its amount, books the change since last month, releases the reserve of a policy no longer covered, and prints the reserves by product. **GLM030** works out the agents' commissions on the premiums collected in the month, forty percent in the first policy year, seven in the second and third, two to the tenth, nothing after, a tenth more for an agent of the second grade and a fifth more for one of the third; charges back the first year's commission on a policy that lapsed or was surrendered in the month in its first or second policy year, forty percent of the first year's premiums paid with the grade's addition; prints them by agent with the agent's branch and the month the agent joined, and writes each agent's totals, commissions and chargebacks, to `OUT\COMMIS.DATE.TXT` for payroll. The close ends with the day's trial balance and the unload.

**GLY010**, at the fiscal year's end, credits a dividend to every covered policy in its third year or later whose product pays one, a rate on the sum, into the policy's accumulated dividends, which the death and maturity benefits pay out. **GLY020** then writes every covered policy's notice, `OUT\NOTICE.DATE.TXT`, for the printer: a page a policy, with the policyholder's address and name, the policy as it stands, its paid-to month, premium, accumulated dividends, loan and cash value, a line for each coverage in force and the total of their premiums.

## Premiums paid and their certificates

Every premium collected is kept in the payment history, `MASTER\PRMHST.IDX`, indexed by policy number and a number within the policy: the first premium of a new policy, a debit that went through, the arrears a reinstatement took at the counter and the premiums the automatic premium loan lent, each with its date in the Western calendar, its amount, the number of premiums it covered and the paid-to month after it. The programs that collect call **GLC050**, which keeps the file.

**GLY030**, in `CERT.BAT`, reads the history through and writes a certificate for every policy with a premium collected from January 1 of the year to the day of the run, `OUT\PRMCERT.DATE.TXT`, a record a certificate for the printer: the amount collected so far and the amount the year will come to if the policy goes on paying, the premiums due by December added. A policy no longer paying is certified for what it paid.

## Where the output is

Under `%GLS_DATA%`:

- `LOG\`: a line for each step, its name, the program and its errorlevel; `DAILY.DATE.LOG`, `BILLING.DATE.LOG`, `RESULTS.DATE.LOG`, `CLOSE.DATE.LOG`, `ANNUAL.DATE.LOG`, `CERT.DATE.LOG`, `UNLOAD.DATE.LOG` and `OPEN.LOG`.
- `OUT\`: what goes to the other systems: the rejects, `NEWREJ`, `CLMREJ`, `MNTREJ`, `LONREJ` and `RESREJ`; the journal; the payments; the transfer request; the commissions; the unloaded master, benefit history and coverage file; the policyholders' notices and the premium certificates.
- `RPT\`: each program's report, `.RPT`, in Japanese, with its counts and amounts, and anything it displayed, `.OUT`.

## The console

The console, `make gondwana-console`, shows the same without opening the files: every business day run with its batch files, counts and worst errorlevel, each batch file step by step, the files each day read and wrote, and any policy's coverages and master record. Its Run button runs the next business days as the scheduler would.

## Return codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
| --: | --- | --- |
| 0 | The step did its job. | Nothing. |
| 4 | The step did its job but rejected some records. | Normal. Check the step's report and its rejects. |
| 8 | The step did nothing: the date was not a date, the month was billed already, or the results file does not agree with its trailer. | Check the date given, or ask the agent for the file again. |
| 16 | The step could not do its job: a file error, or a master it cannot update. | A fault. Read the step's `.OUT`. |

A batch file stops at the first step that ends with 8 or more and exits with its code; nothing after it runs.
